The Negative Consequences

The shortage that fuels UTI's growth is the same one choking its grading capacity

Finding instructors is getting structurally harder. The same nationwide shortage of skilled tradespeople that drives UTI's own enrollment growth also limits the supply of qualified people willing to teach instead of work in the field.
UTI's own Form 10-K names instructor recruitment as a risk to sustaining growth.
Source: Universal Technical Institute, Inc. — Form 10-K, risk factors
North Star Phase II adds 12–16 new campuses and 50–70 new programs through FY2029 — each one widening this bottleneck further.
