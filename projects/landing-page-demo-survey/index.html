How it works

Three steps. You are in charge of the third.

Students submit where they always did. Nothing changes for them until you say so.

1

Submissions land in Eduface

Straight from your LMS assignment, or uploaded as a batch. Your rubric or learning outcomes go in once.

2

Eduface drafts feedback and a grade

It marks the exact passage, writes the comment against your criterion and proposes a score with the reasoning. Where it is unsure, it says so.

3

You approve, edit or bin it

Nothing reaches a student unapproved. Signed-off grades go back to your gradebook. The more you correct it, the closer it gets.

What the lecturer sees

Every comment names its criterion

You can check the reasoning, not just the number. Nothing arrives as a black box, and the grade is a proposal until you sign it.

  • Marks the passage, not the whole paper
  • Score and reasoning per criterion
  • Uncertainty comes to you, flagged

How close it gets to your grade

One pilot at a UK university, June 2026. Six modules, thirteen markers.

~6 pts average gap between its proposal and the lecturer's grade 0100 point scale
~2 pts average gap once the lecturer has tuned it 0100 point scale

That is distance from your grade, not how often it matched exactly. The point is the last number: the more you tune it, the closer it gets, which means you are steering it. Review time per submission in that pilot was two to three minutes.

Common questions

Before you book

Does the AI replace the lecturer?

No. Every comment and grade is approved by a lecturer before a student sees it. Eduface drafts, you sign off. That is not a setting, it is how it works.

What happens in the demo?

Bring one real assignment and the rubric or learning outcomes you mark it against. We run it live and you judge the output. Thirty minutes, on a video call.

How does a pilot work?

One or more modules, one semester, with success criteria we agree on up front. We look at what a wider rollout would need from day one, so the pilot is a step and not a dead end.

Which LMS does it work with?

Moodle, Blackboard, Brightspace, Canvas and most others. Approved grades write straight back into your gradebook.

Do we need a finished rubric first?

No. A requirements list or a set of learning outcomes is enough to start.

How accurate is the grade proposal?

Across 435 assignments and 13 lecturers, it sat about 6 points from the lecturer's grade on average, and about 2 points once tuned. That is distance, not how often it matched exactly.

What happens to student work?

Processed in the EU, never used to train the model, deleted on your schedule. We sign a DPA on request.
