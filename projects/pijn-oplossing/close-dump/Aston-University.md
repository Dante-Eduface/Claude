Hi Natalie,
We have added a new feature called Quick Comments, and it seemed relevant enough to share with you directly.
Quick Comments lets assessors highlight a piece of text in a submission and instantly apply a standardised comment to it.
Most assessors already have their own list of go-to comments they use regularly. Those comments can be added to Eduface and will then appear alongside the default Quick Comments in the panel.
Please find a screenshot of the settings panel attached so you can see exactly where to add your own comments.
The result: a marking workflow that feels a lot less repetitive, and feedback that stays consistent across your entire cohort.
