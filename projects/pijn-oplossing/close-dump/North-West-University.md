Thank you for your enthusiasm in exploring a possible AI-assisted marking solution for WVET 222. After careful consideration, the management team has decided to place this initiative on hold for the time being. This decision is primarily due to the University's transition to a new Learning Management System (LMS), which will be implemented during the second semester and fully integrated across the institution next year.
In addition, the project team responsible for the LMS implementation is also investigating AI-enabled assessment solutions for the University as a whole. At this stage, it is important for us to await the outcomes of this process and to determine which tools and functionalities will be made available through the new institutional platform. Given this broader institutional initiative, it is unlikely that we would receive approval or support to pursue a separate solution until the LMS project team has completed its work and the University's direction regarding AI assessment tools has been finalised.
We sincerely appreciate your interest and willingness to assist us, and we hope to have the opportunity to engage with you again in the future.
