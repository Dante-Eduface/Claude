## Field Day (separate session, never day before football)
Total: ~60 min

1. Warm-up (10 min): dynamic mobility, A/B-skips 2x20m, build-up sprints 3x30m
2. Ladder & footwork (10 min): in-out shuffle, lateral shuffle, icky shuffle - 2 rounds each, 30s rest
3. Plyometrics (12 min): broad jumps 4x3, box jumps 4x5, lateral box jumps 3x5/side - reactive bounce method, 60-90s rest
4. Sprints (12 min): resisted sprints 4x20m, free sprints 100% 4x30m, 90s rest between reps
5. Nickel coverage drills (12 min): backpedal + open hips 4x20m, W-drill 3 rounds, plant and cut 3 rounds, lateral slides 3x15m
6. Finish (4 min): 1 full field sprint 100%, cooldown walk 3 min

Plyometric method: reactive bounce (spring) for lateral box jumps and broad jumps. Static reset for standard box jumps only.
