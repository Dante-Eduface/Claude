# Work — Eduface

**Eduface** is an AI-powered assessment and feedback platform for higher education. It gives students fast, high-quality formative and summative feedback on written assignments, and automates grading workflows for lecturers.

## Products / Revenue Streams
- **Platform** — AI-generated written feedback and grading on student assignments. This is the primary product and primary revenue driver.

## Revenue Stage
- Currently selling **pilots**. Average deal size ~**€10K**.
- Pilots are the on-ramp to full institutional licenses.

## Day-to-Day Tools
- **Close** — CRM (pipeline, leads, notes, follow-ups)
- **Lemlist** — cold outreach automation
- **Google Workspace** — Gmail, Calendar, Drive
- **Framer** — website (eduface.me)
- **Todoist** — task management
- **LinkedIn** — outreach + ads
- **Apple Notes** — quick personal notes
