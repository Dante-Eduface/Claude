# Rubric physics research report

Each criterion receives a grade from 1 to 10.

**1. Theory and research question**
Correct physical theory, accurately stated, with a testable question and hypothesis.

**2. Method and measurement uncertainty**
Replicable measurement set-up, justified data selection, uncertainties correctly determined and propagated.

**3. Calculations**
Formulas applied consistently and correctly, with the right units. A unit error (for example degrees Celsius where kelvin is required) is a serious error.

**4. Interpretation and discussion**
Results critically tested against theory. When a result is impossible, the student checks their own calculation before blaming the measuring equipment.

**5. Conclusion**
Answers the research question, tests the hypothesis, and does not go beyond what the data allow.

**6. Scientific reporting**
Clear structure, correct notation of quantities and uncertainties, careful use of language.
