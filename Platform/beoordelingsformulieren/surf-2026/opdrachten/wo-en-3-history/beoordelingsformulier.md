# Rubric historical essay

| Criterion | Unsatisfactory | Satisfactory | Good | Excellent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Question and thesis** | No clear question or thesis. | Clear question and a thesis that guides the essay. | Thesis is nuanced and well defined. | Thesis is original and sharp, and carries the whole essay. |
| **Historical accuracy** | Factual errors about events, actors or dates. | Facts correct in broad terms. | Facts correct and placed in the right context. | Precise and well documented, including details. |
| **Use of sources** | Primary sources without a reference, or too little literature. | Primary and secondary sources recognisably used. | Sources used critically, with attention to perspective and context. | Sources firmly anchored and critically weighed against each other. |
| **Historiography and counter-arguments** | No other positions discussed. | Another position mentioned. | Different historiographical positions discussed and rebutted. | Debate carefully presented and own position convincingly argued. |
| **Historical thinking** | The past is judged by present-day norms without acknowledging this. | Distinction between then and now largely made. | Conscious distinction between historical context and present-day judgement. | Also explicit reflection on the author's own standpoint. |
| **Structure and language** | Disorganised or many errors. | Logical structure, some errors. | Clear paragraphs and a conclusion that follows from the argument. | Error-free, persuasive and highly readable. |
