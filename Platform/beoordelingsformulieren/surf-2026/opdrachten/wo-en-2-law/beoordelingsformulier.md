# Rubric legal advice

| Criterion | Unsatisfactory | Satisfactory | Good | Excellent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Facts and legal question** | Facts incomplete or no legal question. | Legally relevant facts and a clear legal question. | Also dates and actions relevant to time limits and evidence. | Sharp selection of facts and a precisely defined legal question. |
| **Legal framework** | Incorrect or outdated rules. | Correct articles on consumer sale, conformity and remedies. | Also the presumption, duty to give notice and case law correctly set out. | Complete and up to date, with the European background where relevant. |
| **Analysis and application** | Rules not applied or applied incorrectly, for example termination without first considering repair or replacement. | Conformity, burden of proof and notice applied to the facts. | Also the order of remedies applied correctly. | Also possible defences of the seller rebutted. |
| **Conclusion and advice** | Advice does not follow from the analysis or is legally incorrect. | Conclusion answers the legal question, advice is workable. | Concrete steps with time limits and evidence. | Steps including alternatives if the seller refuses. |
| **Legal writing** | Unclear or careless, references incorrect. | Clear, references largely correct. | Precise wording and consistent referencing. | Error-free, concise and persuasive. |
