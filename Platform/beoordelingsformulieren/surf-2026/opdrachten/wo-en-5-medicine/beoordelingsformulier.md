# Rubric clinical rotation reflection

| Criterion | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| **Description** | Factual, chronological description with the clinically relevant data and times. | Incomplete or lacking the data needed to assess the actions taken. |
| **Critical evaluation of own actions** | Actions are tested against guidelines and priorities in acute care, such as an ECG within ten minutes for chest pain. | The student overlooks important shortcomings in their own actions or presents them as strengths. |
| **Analysis** | Reasons for the actions are explained using the literature or insights into clinical reasoning. | Analysis remains superficial or unsupported. |
| **Professional role** | The student reflects on their own role and authority as a medical student, including supervision. | The action plan or reflection ignores the limits of the student's role. |
| **Action plan** | Concrete, feasible and aimed at the real learning points from this case. | The action plan focuses on side issues or does not follow from the evaluation. |
