# Rubric earn-out analysis in an acquisition

| Criterion | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| **Problem statement and structure** | Explicit central question about the deal structure, logical progression from rationale through valuation and earn-out to sales and conclusion. | No central question, or only a summary of the case. |
| **Strategic rationale** | At least two well-supported reasons for the acquisition, a distinction between cost and revenue synergies, and the consequences of the integration approach. | General reasons without case data, or synergies not distinguished. |
| **Valuation and deal structure** | Purchase price assessed with a justified method, expected value of the earn-out calculated with probabilities and a justified discount rate. | Earn-out added at nominal value, no probability estimate, or a discount rate without justification ("customary" is not a justification). |
| **Figure** | Fully formatted and all values match the text and tables; the conclusion drawn from it is correct. | A value differs from the tables, or the conclusion drawn from the figure does not hold with the correct figures. |
| **Earn-out: incentives and risks** | Measure analysed, conflict of interest between founder and buyer linked to agency theory, dispute risks with appropriate safeguards, supported by literature on earn-outs. | Measure only described, no incentives or safeguards, or no literature. |
| **Sales and integration: MEDDPICC** | Applied to at least two concrete deals, with the change in the sales process caused by the acquisition and the consequences for the earn-out targets. | Only an explanation of the method, or no link to the earn-out. |
| **Conclusion and recommendation** | Answers the central question without new arguments, with a concrete recommendation and a limitation. | No answer, new arguments in the conclusion, or a vague recommendation. |
| **Academic quality** | At least four relevant sources in APA style, clear language, traceable calculations. | Too few sources, concepts misused, or results without calculation steps. |
