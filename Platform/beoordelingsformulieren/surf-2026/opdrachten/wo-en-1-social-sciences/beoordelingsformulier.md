# Rubric bachelor's thesis: social media and sleep quality

Each criterion receives a grade from 1 to 10.

| Criterion | 1 to 5 | 6 and 7 | 8 to 10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Introduction and hypotheses** | Hypotheses do not follow logically from the literature or are not testable. | Clear problem statement and testable hypotheses that follow from theory. | Also a clear gap in the literature that this study addresses. |
| **Design and instruments** | Reliability or operationalisation missing. | Replicable design, validated instruments with reliability. | Also justified choices and a pre-specified analysis plan. |
| **Sample and data handling** | Recruitment, attrition or exclusion unclear, or numbers in the method and tables do not match. | Recruitment, exclusion and attrition described, numbers consistent throughout. | Also representativeness and power discussed. |
| **Analysis and results** | Incomplete reporting or interpretation within the results. | Appropriate analyses, fully reported in APA style, with a clear table. | Also assumptions checked and additional analyses where useful. |
| **Discussion** | Conclusions go beyond what the design allows, such as causal claims from correlational data. | Interpretation in line with the design, main limitations discussed. | Also alternative explanations and concrete further research. |
| **Academic writing and APA** | Frequently careless, or fewer sources than required (at least eight). | Clear academic English, APA largely correct. | Virtually error-free and precisely worded. |
