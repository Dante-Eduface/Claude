# Rubric bachelor's thesis: social media and sleep quality

University, BSc Psychology. Assessment: grade 1 to 10 per criterion. The final grade is the weighted average.

**Note for the marker.** Assess at the level of a bachelor's thesis. The design is cross-sectional and correlational: causal conclusions ("causes", "leads to") are therefore not allowed and should weigh heavily under criterion 5. Check whether the number of participants is consistent across the method, tables and text; unexplained differences are an error under criterion 3 or 4.

| Criterion | 1 to 3 | 4 and 5 | 6 and 7 | 8 to 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Introduction and hypotheses** (15%) | No research question or no theoretical basis. | Literature discussed, but the hypotheses do not follow logically or are not testable. | Clear problem statement, relevant literature, testable hypotheses that follow from theory. | As for 6 and 7, plus a clear gap in the literature that this study addresses. |
| **2. Method: design and instruments** (15%) | Method missing or not replicable. | Design and instruments described, but reliability or operationalisation missing. | Replicable design, validated instruments with reliability, clear procedure. | As for 6 and 7, plus justification of choices and a pre-specified analysis plan. |
| **3. Sample and data handling** (10%) | No information on participants. | Participants described, but recruitment, attrition or exclusion unclear or inconsistent. | Recruitment, inclusion and exclusion, and attrition described; numbers are consistent throughout. | As for 6 and 7, plus a discussion of representativeness and power. |
| **4. Analysis and results** (20%) | No analyses or incorrect analyses. | Appropriate analysis, but incomplete reporting (for example no effect size) or interpretation within the results. | Appropriate analyses, fully reported in APA style, with a clear table. | As for 6 and 7, plus checks of assumptions and additional analyses where useful. |
| **5. Discussion** (25%) | No discussion, or only a repeat of the results. | Results interpreted, but conclusions go beyond what the design allows (such as causal claims) or limitations are missing. | Interpretation in line with the design, linked to the literature, main limitations discussed. | As for 6 and 7, plus alternative explanations, implications and concrete further research. |
| **6. Academic writing and APA** (15%) | Unreadable or without referencing. | Readable, but frequently careless in language or APA. | Clear academic English, APA largely correct, complete abstract. | Virtually error-free, consistent APA, precise wording. |
