# Thesis: social media and sleep quality

University, BSc Psychology, bachelor's thesis. Short version (empirical, quantitative research)

## What will you do?

You will carry out a quantitative study on the relationship between **social media use and sleep quality among students**. You formulate a research question and testable hypotheses based on the literature, collect your own data using validated instruments, analyse the data statistically and discuss the results critically.

This is a **short bachelor's thesis** written as a journal article: 3,000 to 4,000 words, excluding references and appendices.

## Requirements

- Structured as an empirical article: abstract, introduction, method, results, discussion.
- At least eight scientific sources, referenced in APA style (7th edition).
- Report statistics in APA style: test statistic, degrees of freedom, p-value and effect size.
- The study has been approved by the faculty ethics committee; state the reference number.

## The sections

**Abstract.** No more than 200 words: aim, method, main result and conclusion.

**Introduction.** Problem statement, relevant theory and previous research, and a research question with testable hypotheses.

**Method.** Design, participants (recruitment, inclusion and exclusion, attrition), instruments with reliability, procedure and analysis plan.

**Results.** Descriptive statistics and hypothesis tests, with at least one table. No interpretation in this section.

**Discussion.** Interpretation in light of the literature, limitations (especially design and causality), implications and suggestions for further research.

## How will your thesis be assessed?

You will receive a grade from 1 to 10. Each criterion on the rubric receives its own grade; the final grade is the weighted average. The method and results criteria must each be at least 5.5.
