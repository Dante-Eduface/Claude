## Toetsprogramma

Zelfde organisatie als a321, daar volledig met citaten. Kern:
- **Wat:** module-examens (closed-book, open-book casus, oraal), projectweken met schriftelijk verslag plus verdediging, Multidisciplinary (Phase) Exit Exams aan het eind van elke fase (closed-book MC en korte antwoorden, casus met open vragen, mondelinge Q&A), en een graduation assignment van 10.000 tot 12.000 woorden. Inleveren via Turnitin in Moodle.
> "Academic Supervisors are allocated a maximum of 14 hours per student for supervision that includes marking, communication, and attending oral defence."
  bron: EEG Part 8 Bachelor, p. 29 · datum: PDF 2026-09-17 · drager: beoordelaar
> "The GA/FP is marked by the Academic Supervisor of the student and second –marked by another external second marker from another institution/organis ation. Finally, a sample of the marked projects is sent to the External Examiners for their assessment as a matter of quality control and assurance."
  bron: EEG Part 8 Bachelor, p. 26 · datum: PDF 2026-09-17 · drager: beoordelaar
- **Doorlooptijd:** "Results for examinations will be posted on Osiris within two weeks after the examination." (EEG Part 5 MBA, p. 8) · drager: beoordelaar
