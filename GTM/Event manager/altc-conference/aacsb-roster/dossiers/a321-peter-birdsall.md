## Toetsprogramma

- **Wat:** modules met examens (closed-book, open-book casus, oraal), projectweken met "a written assignment submission and an oral defence", Multidisciplinary (Phase) Exit Exams (MEE) aan het eind van elke fase, en een graduation assignment van 10.000 tot 12.000 woorden. Inleveren via Turnitin in Moodle (Wittenborg Online). Bron: EEG Part 5 MBA (PDF aangemaakt 19-05-2026) en Part 8 Bachelor (PDF aangemaakt 17-09-2026).
- **MEE:**
> "The examinations will take the format of ABC examinations, which were traditional at Wittenborg years ago. A: Closed-book Exam – Multiple choice and short answer questions · B: Case study with open-ended questions · Oral Question & Answer of the Case Study in Part B."
  bron: EEG Part 5 MBA, p. 14 · datum: PDF 2026-05-19 · drager: student
  De student legt het examen af. Wie de open casusvragen nakijkt, staat er niet.
- **Hoeveel nakijktijd per scriptie:**
> "Academic Supervisors are allocated a maximum of 14 hours per student for supervision that includes marking, communication, and attending oral defence."
  bron: EEG Part 8 Bachelor, p. 29 · datum: PDF 2026-09-17 · drager: beoordelaar
- **Wie kijkt na:**
> "The GA/FP is marked by the Academic Supervisor of the student and second –marked by another external second marker from another institution/organis ation. Finally, a sample of the marked projects is sent to the External Examiners for their assessment as a matter of quality control and assurance."
  bron: EEG Part 8 Bachelor, p. 26 · drager: beoordelaar
  MBA: "All final graduation assignment/final projects will be second-marked by an external examiner." (Part 5 MBA, p. 11) · drager: beoordelaar
- **Doorlooptijd:** "Results for examinations will be posted on Osiris within two weeks after the examination." (Part 5 MBA, p. 8) · drager: beoordelaar
- **AI-detectie:** "Turnitin, a software that monitors plagiarism and the use of AI writing tools, is available and used by the school" (Part 8, p. 27). Studenten moeten AI-gebruik declareren (Appendix 3).
