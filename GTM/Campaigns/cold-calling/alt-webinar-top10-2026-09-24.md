## Het script

**1. Opening**
> "Hi [first name], it's Dante from Eduface. Have you got a minute?"

**2. Waarom ik bel**
> "I'm calling to invite you personally. We're hosting an online, invite-only session together with ALT and Jisc on the business outcomes of AI marking and feedback for institutions. I sent you an email about it last week."

**3. Waarom jij:** de regel per persoon, zie hieronder.

**4. De vraag**
> "Would you like to join? I'll send you the date and the link straight after this call."

**5a. Ja**
> "Great. Is [email] the best address for it?"
> "Thanks, [first name], looking forward to having you there."

**5b. Twijfel**
> "No problem. I'll send you the details and you can have a look."

**5c. Nee**
> "Understood. Is there someone on your team it would be more relevant for?"

**Vragen ze wie Eduface is**
> "We build AI marking and feedback, developed with Leiden University and Radboud University in the Netherlands. The examiner always keeps the final say. But the session isn't about us, it's about the business outcomes, with ALT and Jisc."

**Voicemail**
> "Hi [first name], it's Dante from Eduface. I'm calling to invite you personally to an online, invite-only session we're hosting with ALT and Jisc on the business outcomes of AI marking and feedback. I'll send you the details by email. Have a good day."

**Niet doen:** getallen uit hun documenten noemen, over de prijs praten, het een kleine sessie noemen (er komen er rond de 60), een datum noemen die je niet zeker weet.
