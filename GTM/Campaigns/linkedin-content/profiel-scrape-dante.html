Beschrijving (About)

"Did you ever have to wait longer than a week for feedback on an assignment you worked hard on?"

That was the question Jeroen van Gessel and Menno Hahury asked me three years ago when they were just starting to build Eduface.

My answer was yes. And I suspect most students would say the same.

"We have the ambition to build an AI feedback tool. Would you want to get on board?"

Three years later, what started as an ambition has become a platform used at universities across the UK and the Netherlands.

My role is to bring Eduface to institutions that are ready to take assessment and feedback seriously. That means identifying the right contacts, understanding what a university actually needs, guiding pilots from start to finish, and making sure the procurement process is as straightforward as possible through the Jisc and HEAnet frameworks.

What keeps me going is what I see when pilots go live. Lecturers who finally have the capacity to give every student the feedback they deserve. Students who receive detailed, consistent responses instead of a few lines at the bottom of a page.

If you are working on assessment, feedback, or AI in education and want to understand what this looks like in practice, feel free to reach out.
