## Post 1 · Dante · P1 Staat en toekomst van toetsen · EN

_Ingekort door Dante 2026-09-20. Goal-alinea, instroom-alinea, vervanging en
studentuitval zijn eruit. Wat overblijft is haakje, betaling/werkdruk, kantelpunt, uren-argument._

More students is a growth plan. More instructors is a hiring problem with a lot more to it than I first thought.

Instructors can earn more in the field than they can teaching it, and carry a lighter workload doing it. So the school is asking that person to take less money, work to a fixed hourly schedule, and then spend the evening grading.

Challenging, but not impossible.

Instructor hours are one of the biggest costs a campus actually controls. Most people look at that line and think about rates and headcount. The more useful question is what those hours are being spent on.

Grading is not why any of them took the job. If that part of the week gets shorter, the instructor gets back the part they came for, which is standing next to the student who is stuck.

Schools treat keeping instructors and keeping students as two different problems. They are the same problem, and it is sitting in the grading pile.

### Alternatieve slotregels
- That one hour keeps two people. The instructor who wanted to teach, and the student who was ready to quit.
- You are not buying back grading time. You are buying back the reason they took the job, and the student who would have left without them.
- One hour off the grading pile. An instructor who stays, and a student who finishes.
- Move that hour and you stop losing people at both ends.

_Vervangen: "That is the same hour working twice. One that keeps the instructor, and one that
keeps the student." Te vaag, de lezer moest zelf uitrekenen wat het oplevert._

## Aandachtspunt

Dante heeft 2026-09-20 gekozen voor de platte formulering: "Instructors can earn more in the
field than they can teaching it." Op BLS-medianen mei 2025 klopt die niet (postsecondary
CTE-docent $72.060 tegenover lassers $53.750, elektriciens $63.190, dieselmonteurs $61.770,
HVAC $68.120). In de bovenste staart klopt het wel, en dat is de groep waar het over gaat.
De gehedgede variant blijft beschikbaar: "Someone who is good enough to teach a trade is usually
good enough to earn more doing it." Het werkdruk-deel van de zin klopt hoe dan ook.

## Post 2 · Dante · P3 Pijn feedback & nakijken · EN

_Geschreven vanuit Dante's oorspronkelijke opzet: het perspectief van de leverancier,
niet van de instelling. "De coole nieuwe tech die je hebt gebouwd wordt niet meer gebruikt"
en "je wordt eruit gezet door een vervanger" zijn de kern._

You can win the deal, pass the IT review, integrate with the LMS, and still be gone in a year.

Here is how that happens. The module leaders get trained so they can train everyone else. Then every lecturer is asked to spend an hour getting used to the tool, and a couple more fitting it into how they work. For some of them that is a way of working they have had for ten years.

So a few of them do it. Most do not. A year later somebody puts the cost next to the usage, decides it is not worth it, and brings in a replacement.

Nothing about the product failed. It just never got started.

What we have learned is that the step that decides adoption is the first one. Setting the thing up. A blank screen and a configuration menu is where you lose people, and you lose them in week one, long before anyone is judging the product itself.

So we took that step off the lecturer. One button, and the course pulls in what is already in the LMS, the slides, the brief, the rubric, the handbook, and builds itself from there.

You do not get renewed on how good your product is. You get renewed on how many people opened it in week one.

### Alternatieve haakjes
- A tool can be fully integrated into the LMS and still be dead within a year.
- Nobody cancels a tool because it did not work. They cancel it because it never got started.
- Every edtech contract that gets cancelled was integrated properly.

### Alternatieve slotregels
- If your tool needs a training session before anyone can open it, you have already lost.
- The first screen a lecturer sees decides whether you are still there next year.
- Adoption is not won in the training session. It is won before it.
