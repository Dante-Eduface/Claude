Start small

Jisc's advice to institutions starting with AI in assessment: begin small, with a pilot. It matches what we have learned across 20+ implementations.

Phase 1A small groupA few lecturers, one module, one use case. The model is tuned to their rubric.
Phase 2Proven use casesWhat worked moves to more modules and more markers.
Phase 3Gradual roll-outThe next use cases are added one at a time, when the institution is ready.
