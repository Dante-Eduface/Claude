## Waarom deze persoon
Route door het organigram: QAHE publiceert een executive leadership team van zeven: Simon Nelson (Chief Executive & Accountable Officer), Tim Slater (CFO), Helen O'Sullivan (Chief Academic Officer), Nick Miller (CCO), Nikki Drake (CTO), Samantha Waters (Chief Governance and Transformation Officer), Stephen Greatrex (Director of People). O'Sullivan is de enige academische functie in dat team en dus de hoogste autoriteit over onderwijs en beoordeling.

Waarom dit de juiste plek is: QAHE levert het onderwijs en het nakijken, de awarding body levert de assessment briefs en de grading criteria. QAA Educational Oversight Review juli 2025, par. 70-71: "Staff are also supported through training in assessment design, marking, and academic integrity policy" en "In partnership arrangements such as that with Ulster University, assessment briefs and grading criteria are typically provided by the awarding body. QAHE adopts and delivers these locally". Par. 73 over de VLE: "used consistently to provide learning materials, track assessment submissions, and return feedback". Schaal: 1.171 medewerkers, waarvan 133 academic staff alleen al op de Ulster-programma's, 6.138 studenten daar in 2024-25.
