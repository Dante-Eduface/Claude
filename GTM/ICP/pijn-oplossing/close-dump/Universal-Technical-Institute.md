### Company Background and Product Development
- Dante Torbed is the first employee of Eduface, a company based in the Netherlands founded three and a half years ago.
- The company was founded to address inconsistent and insufficient feedback from instructors to students due to time constraints.
- Eduface initially attempted using ChatGPT for feedback generation but found it inconsistent in grading and feedback quality.
- The company developed its own AI model trained on academic data with universities in the Netherlands and the United Kingdom.
- The proprietary model ensures persistent, clear, and directional feedback specifically optimized for educational contexts.
### Universal Technical Institute's Current Challenge
- UTI instructors spend two to four hours daily on manual grading of short-answer questions in lab documents.
- Current Blackboard short-answer automatic grading systems fail if student responses are not 100 percent character-perfect.
- Students currently upload fillable PDF lab documents to Blackboard and receive minimal written feedback.
- The challenge was prompted by a program manager teaching a course for the first time who underestimated grading workload.
- UTI serves approximately 20,000 students across 16 programs, with all courses containing hands-on digital labs.
### Desired Solution and Implementation Goals
- Robert Jordan requested integration directly into Blackboard Ultra, UTI's current learning management system.
- The solution should accept rubrics or documents as a knowledge base for grading and feedback generation.
- UTI seeks both formative feedback (immediate student feedback on uploads) and summative feedback (instructor review and adjustment).
- Ideal implementation includes confidence scores indicating AI certainty about grading judgments.
- The AI should provide feedback within lab documents and generate grading assessments based on point-based scoring systems.
### Eduface Product Capabilities and Features
- Eduface provides three types of feedback options: general feedback, pair rubric creation for teachers, and inline assignment comments.
- The platform supports point-based grading and can accept rubrics, answer keys, or contextual documents for model training.
- Eduface integrates with Blackboard and can access approved documents without exposing private information using only student IDs.
- The model works with higher education students at diploma and degree-granting levels, with course durations ranging from nine to eighteen months.
- Dante demonstrated the product showing assignment feedback, rubric-based grading options, and the ability to modify rubric criteria.
### Decision-Making Process and Stakeholders
- Robert Jordan's senior vice president of education must weigh in on the implementation decision.
- UTI's legal and IT teams must review the product for security and integration compliance before implementation.
- If the pilot proves successful, UTI plans full-scale implementation across all programs and students.
- A pilot program with multiple courses across different campuses is planned before considering organization-wide deployment.
- A larger stakeholder demo will be scheduled if leadership is satisfied with initial results.

### Product Demo and Functionality Overview
- Jeroen van Gessel demonstrated the Eduface tool integrated within Blackboard LTI environment using UTI-provided assignment documents as demo materials
- The system automatically syncs grading rubrics from Blackboard to Eduface, eliminating manual upload processes for new programs and locations
- AI generates consistent feedback based on rubric criteria, providing students with explanations of grades and areas for improvement beyond simple numerical scores
- Instructors retain full control by editing AI-generated instructions within the Manage Rubric tab, allowing customization of what the AI model prioritizes
- Completed assignments sync back to Blackboard Gradebook with both grades and AI-generated feedback, maintaining seamless workflow integration
- Spelling and grammar errors are only flagged if explicitly included as rubric criteria, allowing instructors to focus feedback on learning outcomes
### Consistency and Quality Assurance Features
- Statistics dashboard flags inconsistent marking between multiple instructors by showing how often markers edit AI grades compared to the rubric-based AI scoring
- The tool identifies assignments where marker-to-AI grade differences are largest, signaling potential need for calibration sessions between instructors
- Example shown: a marker changed an AI grade from 3/10 to 8/10 (50% accuracy), highlighting significant deviation from rubric specifications
- Two calibration forms exist: direct AI model tuning through prompt editing in the system, and marker consistency flagging through comparative analysis
- Upcoming feature will grade each assignment three times instead of once to provide a consistency score, currently in pilot testing with existing customers
### Technical Integration and Support Structure
- Integration uses LTI 1.3 standard configuration for Blackboard compatibility; Canvas compatibility also available for potential future migration
- Eduface maintains WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility compliance with VPAT documentation available for IT and legal team review
- **Support includes a dedicated customer success manager for urgent inquiries and a ticketing system for bug reporting handled by engineering team**
- Robert Jordan confirmed that VPAT and pricing information are required before presenting to organizational leadership for approval

- UTI proposes 12-week pilot with approximately 120–125 students in HVAC 101 class across four teaching sessions
- First two teaching sessions designated for AI prompt refinement and adjustments; final two sessions to measure impact of improvements
- Primary goal is reducing instructor workload, as UTI instructors face long teaching hours (8–10 hours daily) followed by 3–4 hours of grading
- UTI instructor shortage is exacerbated by industry pay rates exceeding teaching compensation, making workload reduction critical for recruitment and retention
- Accuracy and consistency of grading are essential metrics for pilot success; if pilot succeeds, implementation desired across entire organization

- Student performance summary will identify which students need additional support and which curriculum areas require attention
- End-of-course instructor summary will extract lessons learned and allow curriculum adjustments for subsequent years
- Multi-location data aggregation under development to help identify consistency between UTI's distributed campuses with standardized curriculum
