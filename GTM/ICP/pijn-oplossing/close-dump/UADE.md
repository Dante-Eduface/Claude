### 2026-08-27 | meeting | Eduface X UADE | id=acti_wNAdcq6JRKkkTBmDqE1RqgZxLaYjxMv5IMEVcMbGjrR
Deelnemers: jeroen.van.gessel@eduface.me, lcortinez@uade.edu.ar
SUMMARY:
- Discussed UADE's expansion into AI tool management and need for transparent, objective evaluation platform to reduce teacher grading subjectivity.
- Reviewed challenges with existing grading platforms providing generic feedback and inconsistent marking rather than personalized, accurate assessments.
- Highlighted Eduface's competitive advantage using custom AI model trained specifically on professor feedback instead of generic ChatGPT/Claude models.
- Agreed on two-week free trial in mid-September using actual UADE exams, contingent on Lucas providing detailed feature requirements list within one week.
- Clarified UADE aims to select single grading solution provider, with decision escalation to university directory board after pilot results.
### UADE's Current Initiatives and Team Expansion
- UADE has expanded its team and scope beyond academic projects to include management projects focused on AI and AI tool usage in the university.
- The institution is piloting a spaced repetition learning tool that started in the second semester beginning in August.
- UADE is also working with adaptive learning tools as part of their broader educational technology initiatives.
### Evaluation Process Transparency as Primary Objective
- UADE seeks to make the evaluation process more transparent for students, teachers, and faculty members.
- The institution wants students to perceive exam corrections as objective and based on content and rubrics rather than teacher subjectivity.
- UADE is investing significant effort in academic compliance and wants to digitize paper-based exams onto a platform to support compliance team operations.
### Challenges with Current Grading Platforms
- UADE is currently evaluating three main grading platforms, one from Argentina, one from Portugal, and Eduface.
- The primary challenge across existing platforms is the inability to simulate teacher correction accurately in terms of feedback and marking consistency.
- Tested platforms provide generic feedback that is identical across students rather than personalized feedback based on individual student responses and course materials.
### Eduface's Differentiated Approach
- Eduface developed its own AI model specifically trained for grading and feedback, moving beyond generic ChatGPT and Claude models.
- The proprietary model was trained with universities in the Netherlands and UK to provide more consistent and accurate feedback matching lecturer standards.
- Eduface's AI is trained exclusively on professor feedback rather than internet-wide data, resulting in more specific and contextually appropriate evaluations.
### Current Market Position and Language Support
- Eduface currently has private institutional clients in the United States but is primarily focused on European markets.
- UADE would be Eduface's first Spanish-language implementation, though the platform has been successfully adapted to Arabic, Swedish, and Nordic languages.
- Eduface's model operates natively in English and translates effectively to other languages.
### Partnership Examples and Potential Connections
- Eduface has implemented solutions at universities including Rotabout University in the Netherlands and has medical department clients training doctors.
- UADE expressed interest in connecting with existing Eduface clients among their US partners: San Ignacio University, Florida International University, Arizona State University, Miami Dade College, and Manhattan College.
- Jeroen offered to connect UADE with other universities using Eduface to facilitate experience and best practice sharing.
### Proposed Next Steps and Decision Process
- Lucas will compile a detailed feature requirements list in collaboration with a university colleague piloting the exam project, to be shared within one week.
- A two-week free trial is scheduled for mid-September when Eduface has capacity and when UADE's first exams are occurring.
- The trial will test the platform using actual historical exams from one to two courses to evaluate real-world performance with actual teachers and corrections.
- Lucas is responsible for conducting the pilot test; results will then be escalated to a university directory board for final decision-making.
- UADE aims to select a single grading solution provider rather than implementing multiple platforms.
### Feature Requirements Focus
- UADE requires the platform to correct paper-based exams with strong focus on course-specific content, rubrics, and instructional materials.
- The platform must allow uploading of course texts, rubrics, and subject-specific materials to guide the correction process.
- Success metrics will be defined by UADE's directory board based on organizational priorities and strategy at the time of evaluation.
