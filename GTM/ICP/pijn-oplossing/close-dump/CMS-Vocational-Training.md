Hi Gareth,
Regarding your question about potentially attaching a percentage to indicate AI usage in an assignment: this is something we deliberately do not do.
Our goal with AI detection is not to accuse or detect students of AI usage. The main reason is that reliable AI detection is technically not possible. Even widely used tools such as Turnitin perform very poorly in this area, and from a technical standpoint, accurately detecting AI-generated text cannot be done.
Instead, we use a traffic-light system based on patterns and signals that stand out in an assignment:
  *   Green indicates it is unlikely that AI was used.
  *   Yellow means AI could have been used, as there are some noticeable indicators.
  *   Red suggests a higher likelihood that AI was used.
