## Toetsprogramma
MET (120 ECTS) heeft een verplichte thesis-keten van 31 credits: TH900 Research Methods (3), TH950A/B Prospectus (2+1), TH960 Thesis Writing A (10), TH970 B (2), TH980 C (8), TH999 Thesis Writing D and Defense (5). Nakijken door drie rollen: "the first reader of the thesis in conjunction with the Thesis Supervisor will send off the thesis to the second reader" en "Students are to submit their thesis and defend it orally before a first and second reader". Losse vakken leveren ook schriftelijk werk, bv NT740: "Among the evaluative tools are quizzes, translation work, reading reports, short papers, and an exegetical paper".
