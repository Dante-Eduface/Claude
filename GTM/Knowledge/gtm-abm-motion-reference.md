# GTM / ABM Motion Reference (Eduface)

> Synthesis of two external sources, translated to Eduface and bounded to Dante's scope (up to the Discovery gate / Stage 1 SQL). Sources:
> - Tim Routen (CMO Backbase) podcast on building an AI-native ABM function — the philosophy.
> - Clay blog: "How Clay Runs ABM Campaigns" (Apr 2026) + "GTM Alpha" (Varun Anand, Apr 2026) — the operating playbook + tooling.
>
> Anchor: this feeds **Stage 1 Discovery** of `references/sops/sales-handbook-v1.md`. The ABM machine's only job is to take a named account from cold → aware → in dialogue → SQL (pain + players + why-now + BANT confirmed over email) → **hand off to founders**. Everything below stops at that gate.

## The two-layer model
- **Tim = philosophy.** Everything is ABM. Run a system against a finite named account list, not campaigns at a market. ICP clusters by *title* (multiple wedges per institution). Thin brand layer, deep cluster work. Relationship tactics get the "yes" (podcast, events, peer community, love letters, exec ego/exposure). Pilot the motion on one patch before scaling, no revenue target on the pilot.
- **Clay = playbook + tooling.** Score → tier → coordinated multi-channel campaign → automated handoff sequence → reusable playbook. Plus "GTM alpha": edge comes from unique data + plays competitors can't copy + constant iteration.

## 1. Account scoring (Clay's 3-score model)
Score every account on three dimensions, then filter the big list down to a focused one:
- **Account Fit** — how well they match Eduface's ICP.
- **Engagement** — how actively they're engaging us. **Dynamic, 90-day rolling window** (current behaviour, not a historical snapshot). This is the important one.
- **Potential Contract Value** — what they'd likely spend.

Clay filtered hundreds of thousands → 300 using: fit "Good/Best" + engagement above "Passive" + PCV > $50K.

**Eduface translation:** filter ~130 UK unis (+ NL/IE) down to a focused A-tier. PCV proxy = student population × faculties with high marking load + license potential. Engagement = website visits, webinar/event attendance, LinkedIn engagement, email replies, free-content downloads.

## 2. Tier matrix (Fit × Engagement)
- **Tier 1** (high fit, high engagement): full effort, every channel coordinated. This is Dante's pilot patch (15-25 accounts).
- **Tier 2** (good fit, lower engagement): warm with marketing — thought leadership, events, content. Goal: promote into Tier 1 next cycle.
- **Tier 3**: deprioritise. Not "ignore" — just stop spending scarce time where odds are structurally bad.
- Tiers are a **snapshot per half/quarter**, not a permanent ranking. Re-score and re-tier each cycle.

## 3. Coordinated multi-channel campaign (one message, many channels)
Clay's flagship play against top 300:
- **Physical mailer** — a low-cost, emotional-value gift (a $20 clay kit, not an expensive one) + handwritten personalised note + unique QR code per recipient.
- **Personalised landing page** per recipient (QR-linked), populated with enrichment tokens (their logo, tech stack, sales motion, similar-customer examples). Copy human-written in modular sections; AI picks which sections to show based on what's known.
- **Synchronised LinkedIn/Meta ads** to the same account list, across the whole buying centre, running in the 2-3 week window **before** the package lands — so the brand is already familiar when it arrives.
- **Trigger on delivery confirmation → 4-step SDR sequence:** (1) soft opener email cc'ing the rep, (2) if no reply, call task with a tight script (what was delivered, where, first-15-seconds line), (3) if no connect, manual LinkedIn add + message, (4) final check-in email. The rep's only job is the conversation; everything else is automated.

**Eduface translation (Dante's lane):** this whole flow lives entirely before the Discovery gate. The "gift" should fit HE and the product (something tied to assessment/feedback, emotional not expensive — avoid triggering procurement gift rules). Personalised landing page per target university built in Framer, Clay-enriched (their NSS assessment scores, faculty, AI policy). LinkedIn ads to the buying centre before the touch lands. The 4-step sequence runs in Lemlist + Close; the booked conversation = entry to Discovery.

## 4. Gifting principle (counter-narrative)
- **Emotional value, not monetary value.** Expensive gifts: (a) get flagged by compliance at large/public institutions, (b) create guilt-meetings that were never going to close. A cheap, on-brand, do-it-with-your-family gift earns the *right* meeting — someone genuinely curious.
- **Even non-converters should leave with a positive impression.** Affects referrals, brand in the buying centre, and your second chance 6 months later when they're actually in-market.
- Delivery accuracy isn't make-or-break: a wrong address you correct and resend = ice already broken, relationship not cold.

## 5. GTM alpha = unique data + plays competitors can't copy
- Generic firmographics (size, industry, title) → everyone targets the same accounts at the same time. **Unique, AI-sourced signals** are the edge.
- **How to find your unique data:** study best customers; interview the sales team on what they manually research before a call and why; map where prospects gather and what language they use; "if you had 100 interns, what would you have them do?"; "if you could reach a company at one specific moment, when?"
- **Signal + timing.** Signals have short windows. Act fast; refresh continuously, not monthly/quarterly.
- **Plays compound through iteration.** No play lasts forever. Hypothesise → test → learn → iterate faster than competitors copy you. Surface-level personalisation (mentioning someone's college) stops working fast.

**Eduface unique-data signals worth building in Clay (high alpha for UK HE):**
- Latest **NSS scores**, especially the *assessment & feedback* question block (directly tied to the product pain) — low/declining = burning need.
- **TEF rating** and any recent drop.
- New **AI / academic-integrity policy** published, or a public statement on AI in assessment.
- New **PVC Education / DVC / Director of Quality** hire (new leader = mandate to change).
- **UCU marking & assessment boycott** / industrial action history (acute grading-load + turnaround pain).
- Large cohort sizes in high-marking-load faculties (business, law, nursing, psychology).
- **Jisc/CHEST** membership (procurement path already exists).
- OfS regulatory pressure, external examiner criticism, financial-sustainability warnings.

## 6. Reusable playbook + GTM engineering
- First build is real work; once it's a **reusable playbook**, re-running is ~20 min of setup. Energy then goes to the decisions that matter: **who to target, what to say, what creative earns engagement** — not tooling.
- **GTM engineering** = centralise data + tooling + sales knowledge so a winning play scales instead of dying in one rep's head. Goal: no manual data entry/research; the rep only does high-value conversations.
- For Eduface scale: don't over-build. Clay + Close + Lemlist + Framer is the engine. The "playbook" is a documented, repeatable flow Dante can re-fire each cycle.

## What to copy vs not (Eduface reality check)
- **Copy:** 3-score model, Fit×Engagement tiering, coordinated channels with ads-before-touch, automated handoff sequence into one booked conversation, emotional-value gift, unique-data signals, iterate-and-document into a playbook.
- **Don't copy:** Clay/Backbase headcount and self-built GTM OS. Dante is one person up to the Discovery gate. Use off-the-shelf tools, keep the patch small, re-score each cycle.
