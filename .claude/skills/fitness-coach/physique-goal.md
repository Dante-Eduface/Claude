# Physique Goal - Greek God V-Taper

Aesthetic layer on top of the training program in `athlete-profile.md`. Goal: as athletic as possible with a **V-taper** = wide shoulders + wide upper back, tight waist. The inverted triangle. It does NOT replace the schema, it just tells you where to push.

## The one number that matters
**Shoulder-to-waist ratio (SWR).** The V-taper look is almost entirely this ratio. Golden/Adonis ideal ~1.618. Everything below ladders up to raising it: widen the top, keep the bottom tight.

## Targets

Two ways to hit SWR 1.6, realistic path is a mix of both:
- **Build the top:** shoulders 120 → ~125 cm (lateral delts + upper-back/lat width)
- **Trim the middle:** waist 79 → ~75 cm (lean out, don't bulk the core)

## Training priorities for the V-taper look
Ranked. This is the aesthetic lens on the existing schema, not a new program.

1. **Lateral delts** — the #1 V-taper muscle, they set your width from the front. You already run cable lateral raises 2x/week (6 sets). This is the muscle to add volume to if you add anything. Push toward 9-12 sets/week, high reps (15-20), constant tension, cables > dumbbells for the bottom range.
2. **Lat width** — the taper's outer sweep. Pull-ups (you're at +20kg, strong) build width when you cue "elbows down/wide" and go full stretch. Add a wide-grip lat pulldown or straight-arm pulldown if you want more width bias vs thickness.
3. **Rear delts / upper back** — 3D shoulders + posture that makes the V read. Face pulls + rear delt fly already in. Keep them.
4. **Upper chest** — incline work adds width and "shelf" up top without overbuilding the lower chest. Your incline DB press is already your most-logged lift, and Upper B leads with it. Keep it moderate: upper chest earns its spot, lower chest does not.
5. **Keep the waist tight** — this is mostly diet + not overloading the obliques. Anti-extension core work (ab wheel in Upper A, hanging leg raise in Upper B) is fine, it doesn't blow the waist out. AVOID heavy loaded side bends / weighted oblique crunches / heavy standing rotations under load = those thicken the waist and kill the V. Stay lean; abs and a small waist come from bodyfat, not more ab volume.

## What NOT to do (protects the V-taper)
- No heavy weighted oblique/side-waist work → thick waist ruins the taper.
- Don't chase lower-chest mass → widens the bottom of the torso, works against the V.
- Don't neglect lateral delts to add more pressing → pressing builds front delts (already covered), lateral delts are what actually make you look wide.
- Don't bulk hard (this is the whole point of *lean* bulk) → extra bodyfat sits on the waist first, drops the ratio. Lean gain / recomp given you're already at 79cm waist.

## Diet note
Full targets live in `nutrition-profile.md`. The short version: 3,100 kcal, 175-180 g protein, lean bulk at 0.2-0.3 kg/week. The V-taper is a ratio and the waist half of that ratio is 80% kitchen. You can't out-train a soft midsection, and at 10.2% bodyfat there is no reason to bulk fast.

No conflict.
