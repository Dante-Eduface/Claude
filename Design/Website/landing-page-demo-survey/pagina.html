Questions

The things people ask before they book

If yours is not here, ask it in the call. Thirty minutes, your rubric, one real assignment.

What happens in the demo?

Thirty minutes. You bring a rubric and one assignment you have already marked, and we run it through the model live so you can see how close the proposal lands to your own judgement. No slides.

Does the AI decide the grade?

No. The model proposes a comment and a score per criterion, and the marker approves, edits or discards each one. Nothing reaches a student before a lecturer has signed it off.

How accurate is the proposal?

In the Bath Spa University pilot of June 2026, across 435 assignments, 6 subjects and 13 markers, the proposal sat on average about 6 points from the marker's own grade. For markers who had spent time instructing the model, that dropped to about 2 points. That is distance from your grade, not a percentage of cases where we agreed.

Does it work with our LMS?

Yes. Eduface integrates with Moodle, Blackboard, Brightspace, Canvas and other systems. Students submit where they always did, and the approved mark is written back to the gradebook.

Do we need a rubric?

The Paper Grader is grounded in the rubric and does full rubric scoring, so if you have one, we use it. If your programme works from learning outcomes or a set of requirements instead, bring those to the demo and we will look at what the model needs.

Is this allowed under the AI Act?

Eduface meets the requirements of the AI Act. The human sign-off is not a setting you can switch off, it is how the product works.

What does a pilot look like?

One programme, one assessment round, your own markers. The point is to see the difference on work you have already graded, so you are comparing against something real. We work out the scope in the call.
